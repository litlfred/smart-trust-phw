    Security and privacy are part of each process in this guide, not a separate layer:

    • Consent is enforced where the data is held. The VHL Sharer enforces the consent in its own Consent Registry (IHE PCF [ITI-108]). A copy of a consent carried inside a document is evidence only. See Process A and Process C.
    • No silent permit. A consent decision is permit, deny or unknown. A missing, expired or unreachable consent never gives permit.
    • Trust is checked against a Trust Anchor. Signatures on links and certificates are verified against the trust list of an abstract Trust Anchor ([ITI-YY2]). The guide does not depend on one particular anchor.
    • Decisions are recorded. Each access decision is recorded as W3C PROV-O provenance that cites Consent.id and Consent.meta.versionId. Process G covers audit under a data sharing agreement.
    • Revocation. Process H covers revoking and suspending access.
